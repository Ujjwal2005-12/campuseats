\# CampusEats — System Brief



\## What



CampusEats is a campus food ordering system designed to make ordering food from campus canteens and food vendors easier for students. Students can browse available food items, view menus, add items to a cart, place orders, and track the status of their orders. Vendors can manage their menus and process incoming orders. The system reduces waiting time at food counters and provides a clear way for students and vendors to manage food orders.



\## Who



The main users of CampusEats are:



\* \*\*Students:\*\* Browse menus, select food, place orders, make payments, and track orders.

\* \*\*Food Vendors/Canteen Staff:\*\* Manage food items, receive orders, accept or reject orders, and update order status.

\* \*\*Campus Administrator:\*\* Manage users, vendors, menus, and monitor the overall system.



\## Nouns



The nouns represent the main things, resources, or entities in the CampusEats system.



\* \*\*Student\*\* — A campus user who orders food.

\* \*\*Vendor\*\* — A campus canteen or food provider.

\* \*\*Administrator\*\* — A user who manages the system.

\* \*\*Menu\*\* — The collection of food items offered by a vendor.

\* \*\*Food Item\*\* — An individual item that can be ordered.

\* \*\*Cart\*\* — A temporary collection of food items selected by a student.

\* \*\*Order\*\* — A request made by a student for one or more food items.

\* \*\*Order Item\*\* — A particular food item and quantity within an order.

\* \*\*Payment\*\* — The payment associated with an order.

\* \*\*Pickup Slot\*\* — A selected time for collecting an order.

\* \*\*Notification\*\* — A message informing users about order or system updates.



\## Verbs



The verbs represent the main actions or tasks that CampusEats supports.



\### Student actions



\* \*\*Register\*\* — Create a CampusEats account.

\* \*\*Login\*\* — Authenticate into the system.

\* \*\*Browse\*\* — View available vendors and food items.

\* \*\*Search\*\* — Find a particular food item or vendor.

\* \*\*View Menu\*\* — View the menu of a vendor.

\* \*\*Add to Cart\*\* — Add a food item to the cart.

\* \*\*Remove from Cart\*\* — Remove an item from the cart.

\* \*\*Place Order\*\* — Submit the selected food items as an order.

\* \*\*Make Payment\*\* — Pay for an order.

\* \*\*Cancel Order\*\* — Cancel an eligible order.

\* \*\*View Order\*\* — View order details.

\* \*\*Track Order\*\* — Check the current status of an order.



\### Vendor actions



\* \*\*Manage Menu\*\* — Add, update, or remove food items.

\* \*\*View Orders\*\* — See orders received from students.

\* \*\*Accept Order\*\* — Accept an incoming order.

\* \*\*Reject Order\*\* — Reject an order when necessary.

\* \*\*Update Order Status\*\* — Change an order from accepted to preparing, ready, or completed.

\* \*\*Mark Order Ready\*\* — Indicate that an order is ready for pickup.



\### Administrator actions



\* \*\*Manage Users\*\* — Manage student and vendor accounts.

\* \*\*Manage Vendors\*\* — Add, update, or remove campus food vendors.

\* \*\*Monitor Orders\*\* — View and monitor orders across the system.

\* \*\*Manage System\*\* — Maintain the overall CampusEats platform.



\## Summary



CampusEats connects students with campus food vendors through a simple ordering workflow. The main resources are students, vendors, menus, food items, carts, orders, payments, and pickup slots. The main operations involve browsing, searching, ordering, paying, tracking, and managing food services. These nouns and verbs provide a foundation for designing the database and HTTP API of the CampusEats system.
